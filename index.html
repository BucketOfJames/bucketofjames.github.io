Manifestos

The people who hate me are proof that I'm doing the right thing.

Do you know why just about all the powerful people and almost any figure of authority are so stupid? It's because people hate those who are smarter and more competent than they are. And the masses are generally fairly stupid; the average person is not very smart. This is supported by the Pratfall Effect - a phenomenon where highly competent individuals become more likeable after making a mistake.

Life is actually very simple. If I want something I can always just ask for it. 9 times out of 10 I'm the one overcomplicating shit. So are you, probably.

Who cares what the answer is?

If the good die young, most of you might live forever.

I'm a conceptualist, so I like to think about the future we could have and the potential of humanity, but the hard truth is that the human nature will not let us prevail. We are self-destructive and destined to doom. I'm not being pessimistic, I try my best and I hope for the best. But... c'mon. People fucking suck.

Y'know how they say "You need to love yourself before you can love someone else"? I confidently disagree - I love so deeply that I actually forget to hate myself.

Aren't being bored and being crazy essentially the same? Both have different levels to them, and I am quite certain they overlap more than we might think.

"If they wanted to, they would." is genuinely one of the worst mentalities you can have in almost any relationship. Everyone has things they want to do but won't. I have so many things I (really) want to do but I just don't, I don't for no fucking reason. We are humans, for cryin' out loud - if you want something just ask. And if they keep ignoring that, then you can start considering apathy.

What, you think you're fucking better than me?

Well... you probably are. Not that I care though.

The day we started seeing nudity as something sexual and perverse, instead of the truest form of purity, was the day society was doomed.

One of the reasons I won't kill myself is pure, distilled spite. Not <em>just</em> spite, but defiance too. I am choosing to exist as an act of resistance against the people I know full well would feel some sort of happiness from my <em>absolute absence</em>. Well, fuck them, I win.

While thinking about this, as one does, I decided to look it up. And apparently it's actually a recognized concept in suicidology. It is sometimes called "spite-based survival" or "outliving your enemies." And, somewhat paradoxically, it is one of the most effective short-term protective factors against acting on suicidal thoughts because the motivation is visceral and immediate (anger).

I still consider my main reasons for not succumbing to suicidal ideation to be happiness, hope and love. As abstract as they are.
